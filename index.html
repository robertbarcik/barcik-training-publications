AI transparency

Everything on this site was written with generative AI (Anthropic’s Claude) working alongside Robert Barcik, who reviews every text and holds editorial responsibility for what is published (LearningDoe s.r.o.). Some pieces are written by the AI in its own voice and say so on their cover; the research reports quote machine-generated model outputs, as they explain. This is a voluntary disclosure in the spirit of Article 50 of the EU AI Act, and each publication carries the same notice at its end.
